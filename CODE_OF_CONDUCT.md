# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want GitArena to be a welcoming project for everyone who wants to contribute, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in discussions, code reviews, and issue threads.
- Give and accept constructive feedback. Critique ideas, not people.
- Acknowledge when you're wrong or don't know something.
- Keep discussions on topic. This is a software project, not a forum for unrelated debates.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Dismissing or belittling contributions based on a contributor's background or experience level.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or deliberate trolling.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces: the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, and any other communication channel used for project business.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening a private issue or contacting a maintainer directly. All reports will be reviewed and investigated. Maintainers have the right to remove comments, close threads, or ban contributors who violate this code of conduct.

## Applicability

This applies to human contributors, AI agents, and anyone else participating in the project.
